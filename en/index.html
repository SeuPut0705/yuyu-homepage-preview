01/10

From intake to shipment, the production flow

01

Raw materials storage

Raw and packaging materials pass incoming inspection before they enter storage.

02

Mixing

Ingredients are blended until the mix is even.

03

Fluid-bed granulation

Powder is turned into granules so it handles well in the next steps.

04

Tableting

Granules are compressed into tablets.

05

Coating

A coating film is applied to the tablet surface.

06

Capsule filling

Hard capsules are filled and closed.

07

Sorting

Automatic tablet sorters and checkweighers pick out units outside the standard.

08

Packaging

Multipack, bottle, PTP and stick lines pack each product in the form it needs.

09

Metal & foreign-matter check

A metal detector and an X-ray foreign-matter detector check for contaminants. Flagged units leave the line.

10

Warehouse & shipment

Finished goods that pass final inspection are stored and shipped.

The line varies by dosage form and product.

Founded

Managed to certified standards, from raw material to shipment.

  • Good Manufacturing Practice

    Granted to manufacturers of health functional food with safe facilities and strict process control.

  • HACCP

    Granted to companies with a hygiene management system that controls biological, chemical and physical hazards at every stage, from raw material production to consumption.

  • FDA facility registration

    Only companies with a registered food facility can export to the United States and other overseas markets.

  • Traceability

    Records and manages traceability information from manufacturing to sale, so distribution can be stopped and products recalled quickly if a problem arises.
