R&D

Our in-house research institute studies functional ingredients and dosage forms for health functional food.

Material research

  • Transferring technology for functional materials for anti-diabetes, blood circulation and stress improvement

Dosage form research

  • Developing dosage forms for health functional and general food: tablets, powders, hard capsules, soft capsules and pills
