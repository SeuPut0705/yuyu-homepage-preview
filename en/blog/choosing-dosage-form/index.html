Formulation & packaging

How to choose a dosage form for a health supplement

Choose the form by looking at the target consumer, the nature of the ingredient and storage and distribution conditions together. Tablets, powders, liquids and jellies each suit different ingredients and packaging.

Written by
YUYU Healthcare R&D · Researcher
Published
2026.10.20
Updated
2026.10.31

Start with who will take it

Children, older adults and athletes need different sizes, flavors and serving amounts.

Check the ingredient

Ingredients sensitive to heat, moisture or oxygen suit protective combinations such as tablets, capsules or aluminum packaging.

Consider packaging and distribution

The same ingredient costs and keeps differently in sticks, pouches or bottles, so form and packaging are decided together.

Frequently asked questions

Can the form be changed later?

Yes, but stability tests and labeling must be checked again, so it is best decided at the start.

Are small runs possible?

Minimum quantities depend on the form. We explain the criteria for each form when you inquire.
