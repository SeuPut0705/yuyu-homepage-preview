ODM/OEM

ODM covers product planning, development, production, quality control and shipment; OEM produces and delivers to the client’s specifications.

Strengths

  • Selected ingredients

    We develop and propose ingredients that fit market trends, including distinctive natural materials.

  • Quick response

    A culture that puts client needs first, and fast communication within the team.

  • Certifications

    We hold GMP, HACCP, FDA facility registration and traceability certifications.

  • One-stop service

    Trend analysis, product planning, development and production in one place.

PLAN

Five-step process

  1. 01

    Plan

    We settle the dosage form, specification, timeline and ingredients together.

  2. 02

    Develop

    We design the formula and check appearance and taste on prototypes.

  3. 03

    Produce

    Weighing, blending, filling and packing, each step recorded and every lot traceable.

  4. 04

    Quality

    Inspection from incoming raw material to finished goods, with the results on record.

  5. 05

    Ship

    Shipped to the packaging spec and destination, and traceable after shipment.

Dosage form and packaging specifications are in the manufacturing capability catalog.
